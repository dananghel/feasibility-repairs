\section{Use of AI assistance}\label{app:ai}

We used Claude (Anthropic) in preparing this paper: for literature search and for positioning the
results against prior work; for formatting, \LaTeX{} and the build of this manuscript; for
checking proofs and searching for counterexamples to our own statements; and for routine
calculations. The assistance also contributed to several of the results, their
proofs and the constructions behind them, to the released code, and to the organisation and
wording of the manuscript. Every theorem statement and every experimental number was independently
recomputed in the released code. The author takes full responsibility for the correctness and
originality of all content, including the references.
